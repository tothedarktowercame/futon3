# pattern-mining — process patterns for the campaign itself

**Created 2026-08-13 on Joe's instruction, during the sort of the mined
patterns into content and process.**

## What belongs here

Patterns about **how this work is done**: mining, gating, review, verdicts,
evidence, dispatch, measurement. They are earned from the campaign's own
transcripts and they are useful to the agents running the campaign — the
captain, the conductor, the reviewer, the helpers.

## What must not happen

**These patterns must not enter the mathematics experiment's retrieval
pool.** That is a measurement requirement, not a matter of taste.

The V3 programme (`futon3c/holes/excursions/E-memory-whitepaper-v3-programme.md`)
classifies memories as **substitutive-content** vs **regulative/practice**,
and **P2's falsifier turns on the ratio between them** — "regulative stays
≥80% of fingerprinted uses ⇒ the promotion investment thesis is wrong."

So if a process pattern sits in a math runner's pool and surfaces
accurately, it produces a regulative hit caused by *pool composition*
rather than by the memory being load-bearing — and P2 counts it. Joe:
"accurate recall of them would be bad news." Correct, and the reason is
that it corrupts the programme's headline prediction.

Scoping should ultimately be a store predicate (`:domain`), not a
directory convention, so it cannot be bypassed by filing a pattern one
level over — which is exactly how the mined math patterns ended up
outside the `math-informal*` boundary in the first place. See
`futon3c/docs/pattern-retrieval-architecture.md` §4 and §9.

## OPEN QUESTION for Joe: what is the admission standard here?

The math lane's gate is: **review, then stage-3 teachability assay, then
register** (runbook step 5, amended 2026-08-12). The assay is defined as
"re-run a SOLVED problem on zai-1 with and without the deposit and compare
trajectories against known ground truth."

**That assay is inapplicable to a process pattern.** There is no solved
math problem whose route a gate-design pattern should change.

Three candidate standards, none adopted:

1. **Review only.** These are out of the experiment by construction, so
   the assay's purpose — protecting the experiment from unvalidated supply
   — is already met. Cheapest; weakest evidence.
2. **Use-in-anger.** Admit on ≥1 documented instance of the pattern being
   applied and changing an outcome in the campaign's own record. This lane
   generates such instances continuously and they are cheap to cite.
3. **A process analogue of the assay.** Give a helper seat a real past
   decision with known outcome, with and without the pattern, and score
   whether it reaches the better call. Closest to the math lane's rigour;
   most apparatus.

Until Joe rules, contents are marked `@status ... NOT assayed` with the
inapplicability stated, rather than silently treated as fully gated.
