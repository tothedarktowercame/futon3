## Shape

Two tiers, following Joe's distinction:

| Tier | Mood | Pattern | Relation to WM |
|---|---|---|---|
| precept | "do not" | precept-of-non-harm | the floor; resources/wm/harm-rules.edn H1-H6 is one instance (Lean C3 refusal) |
| optative | "should" | demonstrable-harm-reduction | soft check: a deliverable says what harm it reduces and how that would be seen |
| optative | "should" | world-to-come-in-the-present | the AIF reading: preferences over future outcomes select present action |
| method | how | non-hatred-answers-hostility | de-escalation when input is hostile or failing |
| method | how | remove-causes-not-symptoms | aim at the cause of conflict, not its occurrence |
| method | how | organize-as-effectively | militancy as discipline and organisation, never force |

## Neighbours (extend, do not duplicate)

- paramitas/* — good-will, equanimity, endurance are the personal practices these assume.
- liberation/{mundane,noble}/right-action, right-intention (harmlessness).
  The precept tier here is close to mundane right action; the optative tier
  goes past refraining, which the liberation set deliberately does not.
- eight-gates/* — energetic formations for engaging tension. Not "justified
  use of force" (Joe, 2026-11-05). Its tripwires harm-is-external and
  escalate-on-failure are the failure modes the method tier guards against.
