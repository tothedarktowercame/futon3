@how own mechanism: designate one owning reader per fact; route every mutation through the fact's owning gate; permit second copies only as pins whose sole reader is a comparator and whose disagreement is a loud fault.
@violation-signature Two readers of the same fact disagree in production; a consumer is found reading a copy that is not a pin's comparator; a mutation path writes shared state without passing the owning gate.
! conclusion: Every fact has exactly one authoritative reader; any second copy is either a pin — readable only by a comparator, disagreement a loud fault — or a defect, even while it agrees.

  + context: A distributed apparatus accumulates stores that each hold a view of the same fact: a roster and a spec, a ledger and a submission store, source on disk and namespaces in a JVM.

  + IF:
    Two or more places can answer the same question.

  + HOWEVER:
    The second copy agrees most of the time, so it is read as authoritative by whoever finds it first — a seat type is not the sort of thing a reader expects to cross-check. When it finally disagrees, the disagreement propagates as a correction of the correct value.

  + THEN:
    Designate one owner per fact. Route all mutations through the owner's gate (never hand-edit a merged registry; a board writes only through its gated transcription). Keep second copies only as deliberate pins: stale by design, read only by a comparator, disagreement surfacing as an alarm that stops the dependent publish.

  + BECAUSE:
    A second source of truth is not a view; it will be believed, and its errors propagate with the full authority of the fact.

    + evidence: APM S2 (2026-08-28): the roster's wrong seat type led an agent to change a correct cast to `glm-5.3`; the provider rejected the model and retry exhaustion stopped the campaign. Corroboration from the wm side, through the pin carve-out: negative-control 10j and the glossary-assurance summary each went red within minutes of their populations moving (2026-09-07/08) and stopped the publish until reconciled — the principle working *through* an apparent violation of its letter.

  + NEXT-STEPS:
    - Inventory the facts your apparatus answers from two places; for each, name the owner or convert the copy to a pin with a comparator.
    - Add the write-side audit: list every mutation path to shared state and check it passes the owning gate.
